Admissions & Enrollment

Join an inspiring academic sanctuary. Follow our streamlined admission pathway, review required documentation, and submit your application online.

How To Apply

Step-by-Step Admission Process

Our transparent, merit-based admission procedure is designed to evaluate your scholastic potential, curiosity, and career drive.

01

Online Application

Fill out the digital application form below with personal, academic, and program preferences. Upload your qualifying marksheets.

02

Merit & Entrance Review

Applications are evaluated based on qualifying board scores or HU-Entrance Test performance for professional PG and Ph.D. programs.

03

Counseling & Offer

Shortlisted applicants attend an academic counseling interview (online or on campus). Provisional admission letters are issued promptly.

04

Enrollment & Orientation

Complete fee payment, verify physical credentials, select hostel/transportation options, and attend the grand University Induction Week.

Required Documents Checklist

  • 10th Standard / Secondary School Passing Certificate
  • 12th Standard / Higher Secondary Consolidated Marksheet
  • Undergraduate Degree Marksheets (For PG & Ph.D. Applicants)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate
  • Community / Category Certificate (If applicable)
  • Government ID Proof (Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID)
  • 4 Recent Passport Size Color Photographs
